# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and instructors publish them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Cancel**:
What a learner does to a purchase they no longer want, giving a reason. Cancelling raises a Refund Request; the purchase is only cancelled once that request is approved.
_Avoid_: Request a refund, Reverse, Return

**Cancellation Pending**:
A purchase whose learner has cancelled it while its Refund Request awaits review. The learner keeps access in the meantime.
_Avoid_: Pending Chargeback, Pending Reversal

**Refund Request**:
The review a cancellation starts: the learner's reason and the purchase, waiting to be approved or declined by a person. A declined Refund Request leaves the purchase active.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
Money sent back to the learner when a Refund Request is approved. The same approval cancels the purchase and ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the learner opens through their bank or card issuer, outside Cadence. It is never the name for a Refund Request or a Refund.
